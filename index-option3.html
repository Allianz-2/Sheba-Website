Who defines whose identity is granted space? In Does My Identity Have Space?, thirteen visual arts students interrogate the boundaries of contemporary South African identity in dialogue with Sasol New Signatures Merit winner Snelihle Maphumulo.

Visiting Sculptor

Learn more about Snelihle Maphumulo

Snelihle Maphumulo (b. 2002, Durban) is a sculptor and Rhodes University Fine Art alumna. A two-time Sasol New Signatures Merit Award winner (2024 & 2025), Maphumulo investigates Zulu tradition, female vulnerability, and spiritual endurance through cured sheep hide installations.

“Tradition is elastic—it can stretch to give every identity space without losing its sacred core.”

Heading Reflection

Engaging with visiting sculptor Snelihle Maphumulo allowed learners to examine how personal identity claims space in South Africa. Through collaborative interview design and tactile 1-day studio production, the students discovered that their voices are not peripheral—they are central to the living tapestry of our culture.
